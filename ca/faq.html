Why are you making this?

I had originally made this using the Super Mario Bros X2 engine because funny. Development was way different back then lol. Now i have moved to GameMaker thanks to some annoying limitations with SMBX2. Now, i have more actual reasons making this including:

  • Getting familiar with GameMaker because i wanna get in the indie game industry. (this is my main reason.)
  • Adding more lore to Calvin and Clyde lol.
  • Some attempt at getting better at my crappy self taught music skills.
  • Getting better at art? i mean i kinda don't wanna try to be as accurate as Nintendo's artists but in this present day economy its either your really accurate or your art is mediocre.
