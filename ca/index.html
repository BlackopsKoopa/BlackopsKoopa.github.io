Embrace Chaos. Know yourself. Have determination.

Calvin & The Chaotic Disaster is a 2D platformer game inspired by a couple of other games of that genre. Run and spin attack through many unique levels, defeat bosses with their own weird gimmicks, and stop the world from being destroyed!



STORY

Clyde informs Calvin that something might be up after reading a book about the legend of the chaos creature, Calvin doesn't believe it... until some weird bolt powered by strange energy strikes and launches the two away from each other. And now, it's the start of a grand adventure...
